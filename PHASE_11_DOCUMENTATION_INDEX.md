## 🎯 What Phase 11 Actually Does

### In 1 Sentence
Transforms 40 raw search results into 12 intelligent, deduplicated products with price context, size info, style recommendations, and persistent caching.

### In 5 Sentences
Phase 11 adds five intelligent features to the fashion scanner:
1. **Fuzzy deduplication** removes similar product duplicates (40→12)
2. **Price intelligence** tags prices as great deals or overpriced
3. **Size parsing** extracts clothing sizes from product titles
4. **Style recommendations** suggests complementary items to wear with scanned item
5. **Persistent caching** remembers results for 24 hours across server restarts

### In 5 Bullet Points
- ✨ Smart deduplication (40 → 12 products)
- 💰 Price context (🔥 good deal, ⚠️ expensive)
- 👕 Size detection (L, Size 8.5, etc.)
- 👗 Style recommendations (what to wear with it)
- 📁 Smart caching (instant repeat scans)
